How it works

Three surfaces. That's it.

1

Set a goal

Name, target amount, deadline, and what "one step" is worth. Takes under a minute.

2

Click your buddy

Every click logs +1 instantly. The pet slides along a bar. A fixed line shows the ideal pace.

3

Glance, don't open

The pet lives on your desktop. You always know if you're ahead or behind without opening anything.

Why it's different

Goals fail when they become invisible.

Ambient, not periodic

Habit apps only push you when you remember to open them. Grow Buddy stays on screen and doesn't disappear.

One click, not one dialog

Most trackers ask you to type a number. Grow Buddy asks you to click a pet. Friction matters.

Ideal pace, not just progress

A single percentage tells you nothing. Grow Buddy shows where you should be, so you can course-correct.

Yours, on your machine

No account. No server. No login. Your data lives in a JSON file you can back up or delete.

Privacy

What leaves your machine.

Free tier

Nothing. Grow Buddy runs offline. No account, no telemetry, no tracking. Your goals live in goals.json in your OS user-data folder. Uninstalling deletes that folder.

Pro tier

Only the numbers you agree to share, and only with people you gave a code to. Your target amount and history stay local unless you explicitly enable sync on a per-goal basis.
