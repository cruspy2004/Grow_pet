## How it works (3 cards)

1. **Set a goal.** Name, target amount, deadline, and what "one step" is worth. Takes under a minute.
2. **Click your buddy.** Every click logs +1 instantly. The pet slides along a bar. A fixed line shows the ideal pace.
3. **Glance, don't open.** The pet lives on your desktop. You always know if you're ahead or behind without opening anything.

## Why it's different (4 cards)

- **Ambient, not periodic.** Habit apps only push you when you remember to open them. Grow Buddy stays on screen and doesn't disappear.
- **One click, not one dialog.** Most trackers ask you to type a number. Grow Buddy asks you to click a pet. Friction matters.
- **Ideal pace, not just progress.** A single percentage tells you nothing. Grow Buddy shows where you should be, so you can course-correct.
- **Yours, on your machine.** No account. No server. No login. Your data lives in a JSON file you can back up or delete.

## Voice / tone rules

- Short sentences. No adverbs when a verb will do.
- Second person; talk to the reader as "you."
- Zero exclamation marks.
- No "revolutionize" / "unlock" / "supercharge" / any startup-brochure verbs.
- If a claim needs a caveat, put the caveat in the same sentence, not a footnote.
