# Later

Ideas worth remembering, not committed to. Some might never happen — that's fine.

## Ambient / social

- Multiple pets on screen at once, one per active goal.
- Ghost of your own past self (last month you) on the bar for solo comparison.
- Group leaderboards for Pro (opt-in per group).

## Pacing

- Milestone / non-linear ideal lines (weekly targets, ramp-up schedules).
- Auto-adjust deadline based on rolling actual pace.
- Predictive marker showing where you'll land if today's pace continues.

## Integrations

- Calendar block: "here's your daily +1 window."
- Bank sync via Plaid/Setu for savings goals (opt-in, adds real complexity).
- Apple Health / Google Fit for step-count-based goals.

## Platforms

- Mobile companion app that syncs via the Pro backend (Pro-only).
- Web dashboard for goal setup on a bigger screen.
- Browser extension that shows the bar in a corner of every tab.

## Content

- Weekly digest email (Pro-only): what you shipped, where you're behind.
- "Grow Buddy" as an accountability community — shared vocabulary, curated Pro groups.
